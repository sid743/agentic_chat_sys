# Information Handling Policy

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This policy defines how company, customer and employee information is classified, accessed, shared and retained.

## 2. Classification

2.1 **Public**: approved for external release.

2.2 **Internal**: for employees only (for example, most policies and org charts).

2.3 **Confidential**: limited to people who need it for their role (for example, customer contracts, financial forecasts).

2.4 **Restricted**: the most sensitive information, including employee personal data such as salary, leave balances and leave history, medical information, performance records, and government identifiers.

## 3. Principles

3.1 Access is granted on a need-to-know basis and for the minimum data necessary.

3.2 Restricted data must not be shared with anyone who is not authorised, including colleagues in the same team.

## 4. Access to HR Data

4.1 **Employees** may view their own HR records, including their own leave balances, leave requests and notifications.

4.2 **Managers** may view leave balances and leave requests of their direct reports only, for the purpose of approving leave and planning work.

4.3 **HR administrators** may view HR data for any employee when required for their role.

4.4 **Directory information** (name, job title, department, work location and reporting manager) is Internal and may be looked up by any employee.

4.5 No one may disclose another employee's leave balance, leave reason or medical information to people who are not authorised under 4.1-4.3.

4.6 **HR systems and AI assistants** must enforce these rules in the same way as people: a request for another employee's Restricted data must be refused unless the requester is that employee's manager or an HR administrator.

## 5. Sharing and Transmission

5.1 Restricted data may only be shared through approved company systems. It must not be sent to personal email accounts or messaging apps.

5.2 Documents containing Restricted data must be labelled.

## 6. Retention

6.1 Leave records are kept for 8 years after the end of employment.

6.2 Conversation logs from the approved HR assistant are kept for 90 days for troubleshooting and audit, and are then deleted.

## 7. Incident Reporting

7.1 Any suspected disclosure of Confidential or Restricted data must be reported within 24 hours to security@example.com.

## 8. Use of AI Tools

8.1 Employees must not paste Confidential or Restricted data into AI tools that are not approved by Information Security.

8.2 The approved HR assistant may process an employee's own Restricted data to answer that employee's questions.

8.3 Documents uploaded to the HR assistant are used only to answer questions in the same conversation.
