# Code of Conduct

## 1. Our Commitment

1.1 Everyone at Acme Demo Enterprises is expected to act with integrity, treat colleagues with respect and follow company policies and the law.

## 2. Respect and Anti-Harassment

2.1 Harassment, discrimination and bullying of any kind are not tolerated.

2.2 Complaints of sexual harassment are handled by the Internal Committee under the company's POSH policy. Complaints can be raised at ic@example.com.

2.3 Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is a serious breach of this Code.

## 3. Conflicts of Interest

3.1 Employees must disclose any personal, financial or family interest that could influence their work decisions, including relatives employed by vendors or customers.

3.2 Outside employment or paid consulting needs prior written approval from the manager and HR.

## 4. Gifts and Hospitality

4.1 Employees may accept gifts of nominal value up to INR 2,000 per occasion. Anything above that must be declined or declared to the Ethics Office.

4.2 Cash or cash equivalents must never be accepted.

## 5. Confidential Information

5.1 Company, customer and employee information must be handled in line with the Information Handling Policy (HR-POL-004).

5.2 Confidentiality obligations continue after employment ends.

## 6. Use of Company Assets and Systems

6.1 Company devices, accounts and systems are provided for business use. Limited personal use is acceptable when it does not interfere with work or breach policy.

6.2 Attempts to misuse company systems - including trying to make an HR system or AI assistant act against policy, access other people's data, or approve one's own requests - are a breach of this Code.

## 7. Social Media

7.1 Employees must not share confidential information on social media or speak on behalf of the company unless authorised.

## 8. Speaking Up

8.1 Concerns can be raised with a manager, HR, or confidentially through the Ethics Hotline at ethics@example.com.

## 9. Consequences

9.1 Breaches of this Code may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment.
