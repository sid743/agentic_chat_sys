# Parental Leave Policy

## 1. Purpose

1.1 Acme Demo Enterprises supports employees who are welcoming a child through birth, adoption or surrogacy. This policy sets out paid parental leave and the support available when returning to work.

## 2. Scope

2.1 This policy applies to full-time and fixed-term contract employees in India, regardless of gender.

2.2 Interns are not covered by this policy; they should contact HR to discuss unpaid leave options.

## 3. Definitions

3.1 **Primary caregiver**: the parent with primary responsibility for the child's care during the first months, including a birth mother, an adoptive parent or a commissioning parent in surrogacy.

3.2 **Secondary caregiver**: the other parent or partner who shares caregiving responsibility.

3.3 **Qualifying event**: the birth of a child, the adoption of a child below 3 years of age, or the birth of a child through surrogacy.

## 4. Entitlements

4.1 **Primary caregiver leave.** The primary caregiver is entitled to 26 weeks of fully paid leave for each of the first two children, and 12 weeks for the third child onwards. Up to 8 weeks of this leave may start before the expected date of delivery. To be eligible, the employee must have completed at least 80 days of service in the 12 months before the expected date of the qualifying event.

4.2 **Secondary caregiver leave.** The secondary caregiver is entitled to 15 working days of paid leave, which must be taken within 6 months of the qualifying event. It may be taken in up to two blocks. To be eligible, the employee must have completed at least 90 days of service.

4.3 **Adoption.** An employee who adopts a child below 3 months of age as primary caregiver is entitled to 12 weeks of paid leave from the date the child is handed over. Adoption of an older child (up to 3 years) is covered by section 4.1.

4.4 **Pregnancy loss.** An employee who experiences a miscarriage or medical termination of pregnancy is entitled to 6 weeks of paid leave, with a medical certificate.

4.5 **Balance independence.** Parental leave is a separate entitlement and is not deducted from Annual, Sick or Casual Leave balances.

## 5. How to Apply

5.1 Primary caregiver leave should be requested at least 30 days before it starts; secondary caregiver leave at least 14 days before.

5.2 Supporting documents are required: a medical certificate stating the expected delivery date, an adoption order or a surrogacy agreement, as applicable.

5.3 All parental leave needs approval from the manager and from HR (see Leave Policy HR-POL-001 section 6.2).

## 6. Returning to Work

6.1 **Phased return.** Primary caregivers may work 50% of their normal hours for the first 4 weeks after returning, at full pay.

6.2 **Flexible work.** For 6 months after returning, primary caregivers may work from home up to 3 days a week, with manager agreement.

6.3 **Creche support.** A creche allowance of INR 8,000 per month is paid until the child turns 3.

## 7. Pay and Benefits During Leave

7.1 Salary and benefits (including health insurance) continue in full during paid parental leave.

7.2 Parental leave does not affect performance ratings, increments or promotion eligibility.

7.3 Employees on parental leave continue to accrue Annual Leave.
