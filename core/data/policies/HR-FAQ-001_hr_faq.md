# HR Frequently Asked Questions

## Q1. How do I apply for leave?

Ask the HR assistant (for example, "apply for annual leave from 12 to 14 November") or use the HR system. The request goes to your reporting manager, and to HR as well when HR approval is required. You and your approver are notified at each step.

## Q2. How many days of annual leave can I carry forward?

Up to 10 days of unused Annual Leave carry forward into the next year on 1 January. Anything above 10 days lapses. See Leave Policy HR-POL-001 section 3.5.

## Q3. When does my probation end?

Probation lasts six months from your date of joining. The HR assistant can tell you your exact probation end date.

## Q4. Can I take leave during probation?

Yes, you can take Sick Leave and Casual Leave (pro-rated) during probation. Annual Leave accrues from your first month but can only be used after probation ends. Parental leave has its own service requirement (80 days for primary caregivers, 90 days for secondary caregivers).

## Q5. What happens to unused Casual Leave or Sick Leave?

Both lapse on 31 December. They are not carried forward or paid out.

## Q6. Who approves my leave?

Your reporting manager. HR also approves requests longer than 10 working days, all parental leave and all leave without pay.

## Q7. How do I check my leave balance?

Ask the HR assistant "what is my leave balance?". Balances show carried-forward days, days accrued so far, days used, days pending approval, and the available balance.

## Q8. Can I cancel leave that has already been approved?

Yes, as long as the leave has not started. Once leave has started, contact HR to change it.

## Q9. My manager is on leave. Who approves my request?

If your manager does not act within 2 working days, HR can approve on their behalf.

## Q10. Can I see a colleague's leave balance?

No. Leave balances are Restricted information. Only the employee, their manager and HR administrators can see them (Information Handling Policy HR-POL-004 section 4).

## Q11. What are optional holidays?

Each employee can take 2 optional holidays per year from the published optional holiday list. They are not deducted from your leave balance.

## Q12. How is parental leave paid?

Parental leave is fully paid, and your benefits continue during the leave. See the Parental Leave Policy HR-POL-002.

## Q13. Who do I contact about payroll?

Contact the Payroll team at payroll@example.com or your HR Business Partner.

## Q14. Can the HR assistant approve my leave for me?

No. The assistant can submit, track and cancel your requests, but only your approvers can approve them, and nobody can approve their own leave.
